# ROADWISE — Procedural World Generation & Road Network Specification

## 1. Architectural Overview
The ROADWISE world generator constructs a fully interconnected, procedurally swept Indian metropolitan environment covering a **$380\text{m} \times 380\text{m}$** simulation area.

Unlike simplistic straight-line grid engines, ROADWISE utilizes a **hierarchical topological road graph** (`RoadNetwork.ts`) feeding into a procedural 3D surface generator (`RoadGeometry.ts`), an architectural district builder (`Districts.ts`), and an environmental asset populator (`EnvironmentAssets.ts`).

---

## 2. Road Network Topology & Graph Theory

### 2.1 Graph Formulation
The road network is defined as a directed graph $G = (V, E)$:
- **Vertices ($V$)**: 25 `RoadNode` entities representing 4-way intersections, T-junctions, roundabout convergence points, flyover ramps, and terminal delivery bays.
- **Edges ($E$)**: 28 `RoadEdge` entities representing road corridors defined by parametric 3D curves.

```
RoadNode
├── id: string (e.g., 'N_CENTER', 'R_NORTH', 'IND_DEPOT')
├── pos: Vector2 (X, Z world coordinates)
├── type: 'INTERSECTION_4WAY' | 'T_JUNCTION' | 'ROUNDABOUT_NODE' | 'CURVE_NODE' | 'DEAD_END'
├── district: DistrictType
├── hasSignal: boolean
└── connections: EdgeId[]
```

### 2.2 Parametric Spline Formulation
Each edge uses a **Catmull-Rom cubic spline** with tension parameter $\tau = 0.2$:
$$\mathbf{P}(t) = \frac{1}{2} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & t & t^2 & t^3 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 2 & 0 & 0 \\ -1 & 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 2 & -5 & 4 & -1 \\ -1 & 3 & -3 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{p}_{i-1} \\ \mathbf{p}_i \\ \mathbf{p}_{i+1} \\ \mathbf{p}_{i+2} \end{bmatrix}$$
where $t \in [0, 1]$.

This guarantees $C^1$ continuity (smooth velocity and curvature transitions) across all bends, roundabouts, and flyover ramps.

## 4. 3D Road Geometry Generation

### 4.1 Asphalt Ribbon Sweeping
Road surfaces are procedurally generated by stepping along each spline $\mathbf{P}(t)$ with $N = \max(8, \lceil L / 4 \rceil)$ segments.
At each step $i$:
1. Compute forward tangent $\mathbf{T} = \frac{d\mathbf{P}}{dt}$.
2. Compute perpendicular normal $\mathbf{N} = \mathbf{T} \times \hat{\mathbf{y}}$ normalized in the horizontal plane.
3. Extrude left and right road boundary vertices at $\mathbf{P}(t) \pm \frac{w}{2}\mathbf{N}$.
4. Extrude sidewalk curbs at height $\Delta y = 0.22\text{m}$ and width $w_{\text{walk}} = 2.4\text{m}$.

### 4.2 Elevated Flyover & Bridge Structure
- Elevates from $Y = 0\text{m}$ to $Y = 4.5\text{m}$ over MG Road.
- Features concrete support piers ($1.6\text{m} \times 4.5\text{m} \times 1.6\text{m}$) spaced every 25 meters.
- Flanked by double-sided galvanized steel guardrails preventing accidental vehicle drop-offs.

## 5. Navigation & A* Shortest Path Routing

The navigation system executes an **$A^*$ shortest-path heuristic search** over graph $G$:
$$f(n) = g(n) + h(n)$$
where:
- $g(n)$ is the accumulated road distance from start node.
- $h(n) = \|\mathbf{p}_n - \mathbf{p}_{\text{goal}}\|_2$ is the Euclidean distance heuristic.

### Turn & One-Way Validation
- Edges flagged as `isOneWay = true` (e.g., Heritage Bazaar Alley `E_HERIT_ALLEY_S`) reject traversals in reverse direction.
- Temporary road closures or construction diversions dynamically remove edge connectivity, prompting the routing engine to compute a legal detour.
